How to Choose the Right Keyboard

When selecting a keyboard for programming, consider the following factors:

  • Split Design: Allows your hands to stay at shoulder width.
  • Tenting: Tilts the keyboard so your palms face each other slightly.
  • Switch Type: Mechanical switches are generally preferred for durability and feel.
